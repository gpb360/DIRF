# Dependency-ordered production tickets

These tickets trace to `SPEC.md`. Execute one ticket per fresh production
context unless the work is trivial and shares the same files.

## T01 — Approve positioning and public proof boundary

**Depends on:** none

**Produces:** accepted `ADR-001-positioning.md`, confirmed pronunciation,
approved public repository URL, and named versus anonymized project decision.

**Done when:** Gate A is recorded and no script relies on an unresolved name or
claim.

## T02 — Reconcile the recording commit

**Depends on:** T01

**Produces:** clean recording worktree, exact SHA/version manifest, passing
repository validation, and raw output for every demonstrated command.

**Done when:** the feature/command matrix is verified against one commit and
the local checkout is not mixing release and unreleased behavior.

## T03 — Table-read and lock episode scripts

**Depends on:** T01, T02

**Produces:** corrected read-aloud scripts with final runtime estimates,
chapters, titles, thumbnail phrases, command callouts, and source callouts.

**Done when:** every line is natural when spoken and every product claim traces
to T02 evidence.

## T04 — Approve voice and audio identity

**Depends on:** T03

**Produces:** approved local narration test, pace, music decision, and
sound-mark palette.

**Done when:** Gate C accepts the narration approach; all audio remains outside
Git and any paid generation remains a separate decision.

## T05 — Finalize reusable visual package

**Depends on:** T01, T02

**Produces:** validated Excalidraw map, 16:9 and 9:16 crops, opener, lower third,
terminal style, comparison lanes, evidence gate, handoff transition, and end
card.

**Done when:** assets are readable at final delivery size and the motion plan
matches `SPEC.md`.

## T06 — Produce episode 1

**Depends on:** T03, T04, T05

**Produces:** final preview, captions, thumbnail A/B options, description, and
five Shorts source markers.

**Done when:** product, visual, audio, caption, and channel-package QA pass.

## T07 — Produce episode 2

**Depends on:** T03, T04, T05

**Produces:** full tutorial preview and support assets.

**Done when:** every command is visible, reproducible, and tied to the exact
recorded commit.

## T08 — Produce episode 3

**Depends on:** T03, T04, T05

**Produces:** fair comparison preview and official source callouts.

**Done when:** each compared method has a best-fit use and no unsupported
winner claim remains.

## T09 — Produce episode 4

**Depends on:** T03, T04, T05

**Produces:** portability and standards preview.

**Done when:** “agnostic” is shown through live discovery and portable output,
with host-specific limits stated.

## T10 — Produce episode 5

**Depends on:** T03, T04, T05

**Produces:** approved multi-project operating proof and advanced governance
section.

**Done when:** internal use is labeled accurately and all project identifiers
are approved for public display.

## T11 — Build the first 25 Shorts drafts

**Depends on:** T06–T10

**Produces:** five Short drafts per episode with related-video targets.

**Done when:** each Short contains one complete idea and passes vertical caption
and safe-area QA.

## T12 — Final release package

**Depends on:** T06–T11

**Produces:** upload masters, thumbnails, captions, descriptions, chapters,
source list, playlist order, and analytics worksheet.

**Done when:** Gate E is accepted for each episode. Upload, schedule, and publish
remain behind separate Gate F approval.

## T13 — Review the 7-day and 28-day evidence

**Depends on:** published work under separate approval

**Produces:** episode and Short findings, one-variable test decisions, and the
next batch of topics.

**Done when:** recommendations use the channel's own appeal, engagement,
satisfaction, search, and conversion evidence rather than generic benchmarks.
