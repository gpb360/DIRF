# Episode 1 expanded production prompt

## Style

Use `#06101e` as the immutable canvas, `#e8f1ff` for primary type,
`#4f8cff` for the active route, `#5ee2ad` only for earned proof, and `#f6b94b`
for decisions. Display voice: Arial Black. Human explanation: Georgia.
Operational metadata: Courier New. Mood: a mature technical control room, calm
under pressure, exact without sterility.

## Rhythm

DISRUPT — inspect — route — destabilize — PROVE — assemble — breathe — act.

## Global direction

- Each scene carries a deep background field, a working artifact in the
  midground, and foreground proof details.
- Generated plates drift slowly behind native typography and reconstructed
  evidence surfaces.
- Hard cuts separate the three failures. Cobalt wipes reveal the DIRF model.
- No invented metrics, customer logos, monitoring claims, or fake product UI.

## Scene worlds

1. Archaeological site: we are already deep inside an overgrown chat history.
   Fragments drift past camera until a blank handoff becomes the only stable
   object. Transition: hard focus pull.
2. Wrong route: a galaxy of capabilities surrounds one vague task. Three
   relevant skills lock onto a cobalt route; everything else falls quiet.
   Transition: lateral route wipe.
3. Context drift: two worktree lanes split across the frame with conflicting
   notes. A repository-keyed handoff remains centered and calm. Transition:
   split collapses into a vertical proof line.
4. Completion fog: a giant unsupported DONE stamp loses opacity. Three distinct
   verification rows assemble; one becomes green only after evidence lands.
   Transition: amber gate sweeps to green.
5. DIRF model: Route, Record, and Finish line assemble asymmetrically around a
   central evidence spine. Camera makes a controlled push toward the complete
   operating loop. Transition: measured cobalt wipe.
6. Boundaries: four near-monochrome title cards arrive as quiet institutional
   statements. Amber identifies authority that stays with humans and systems.
   Transition: clean hard cut.
7. First command: a terminal command types on at readable scale. A route draws
   across the visual field and ends at a verified handoff card. Resolve on the
   next episode title.

## Recurring motifs

Cobalt route line, three-zone handoff, evidence stamp, repository registration
marks, and a quiet `01 / DIRF` episode index.

## Negative prompt

No equal card dashboard, no tiny web UI, no gradient headline text, no purple
cyberpunk, no confetti, no fake live-monitor state, and no unsupported success
claim.
