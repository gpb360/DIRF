# Episode 1 audio and cue sheet

## Narration status

Narration recordings are private, local-only production inputs and ignored by
Git. Record the 60-second test after the campaign decision gate, then commit
only the resulting timing notes and cue map.

## Music pass, later

Once narration is approved, keep it dominant. A restrained, lyric-free
technical pulse can sit near
-27 LUFS under speech, duck 3–5 dB on dense phrases, and fade before the final
command resolves. Music sourcing and licensing remain a separate approval gate.
