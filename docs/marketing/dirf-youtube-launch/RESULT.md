# DIRF YouTube launch research result

## Result

The five-video DIRF launch is specified and recording-ready at the planning
level. The package contains current market research, a positioning ADR, a
five-episode specification, five voiceover-ready scripts, a 25-Short extraction
system, a production and approval workflow, dependency-ordered tickets, and a
reusable visual map.

## Key decision

DIRF should be marketed as the preflight, routing, continuity, and evidence
layer for agent work:

> Keep your agent stack. Give it a route, a record, and a finish line.

This position is more accurate and defensible than claiming DIRF replaces Spec
Kit, BMAD, GSD, Superpowers, AGENTS.md, Agent Skills, or MCP. The series shows
how those layers can cooperate.

## Audience

Lead with multi-agent operators: developers, technical founders, consultants,
agency leads, and engineering teams already using multiple hosts, skills,
worktrees, or project workflows. Their immediate job is reliable routing and
continuity, not access to another model.

## Five episodes

1. Why agent work falls apart between sessions
2. Full DIRF walkthrough from task to verified handoff
3. DIRF beside Spec Kit, BMAD, GSD, and Superpowers
4. Agent Skills, AGENTS.md, MCP, and agnostic routing
5. Multi-project continuity, worktrees, portfolio state, and governance

## Evidence

- Current official product and standards sources support the market shift toward
  specs, skills, subagents, portable instructions, and tool connections.
- Official YouTube guidance supports problem-led packaging, immediate promise
  fulfillment, retention review, and format-specific analytics.
- Live DIRF evidence routed a PR-review task into three installed capabilities,
  reconciled canonical state, and rolled up nine registered projects in the
  research-day portfolio snapshot.
- Current `origin/main` documents the route/state/evidence mechanisms and the
  newer governed-execution layer.

## Validation

- Five script files are present.
- Script voiceover runs from 888 to 1,077 words before live-demo pauses, matching
  the planned 8–12 minute edited range.
- The banned marketing-copy phrase scan returned no matches.
- Excalidraw JSON parses successfully.
- The official Excalidraw renderer timed out on its remote module twice. The
  equivalent SVG fallback rendered successfully and the PNG passed two visual
  inspection passes. The `.excalidraw` render remains explicitly unverified.
